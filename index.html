About NEXUS VAULT

Your entire crypto world. One powerful view.

One unified crypto workspace.

NEXUS VAULT is designed to help users organize their wallet connections and understand their combined digital asset portfolio. The platform uses user-approved wallet connections and public blockchain information to display balances and portfolio values.

Users retain control of their wallets. Private keys and recovery phrases are never required by the portfolio dashboard.

Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers about NEXUS VAULT, wallet connections, privacy and your portfolio.

What is NEXUS VAULT?

NEXUS VAULT is designed to bring information from your different crypto wallets into one unified portfolio view.

Do I need to share my recovery phrase?

No. Never share your seed phrase, recovery phrase or private keys with this website or anyone claiming to represent it.

Can I connect multiple wallets?

The platform is being developed to support multiple wallet connections and display each wallet alongside a combined portfolio total.

Does NEXUS VAULT hold my cryptocurrency?

The intended portfolio experience is non-custodial: it displays wallet information without taking custody of your assets. Always review wallet approval requests carefully.

Why can I choose a display currency?

USD is the base currency. You can choose a local display currency to see equivalent portfolio values. Currency selection does not require access to wallet credentials.

Are wallet connections live yet?

The current version includes the wallet directory and interface. Secure wallet-provider connections and live balances are the next development stage.
