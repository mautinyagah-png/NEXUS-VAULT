About NEXUS VAULT

A unified approach to wallet management.

One workspace. Multiple wallets.

NEXUS VAULT is a wallet management interface concept designed to bring supported wallet connections into one organized workspace. Our goal is to provide a clear, convenient and user-controlled experience across different blockchain ecosystems.

Wallet connections require explicit approval from the wallet owner. NEXUS VAULT does not need your recovery phrase or private keys.
