About NEXUS VAULT

Your entire crypto world. One powerful view.

One unified crypto workspace.

NEXUS VAULT is designed to help users organize their wallet connections and understand their combined digital asset portfolio. The platform uses user-approved wallet connections and public blockchain information to display balances and portfolio values.

Users retain control of their wallets. Private keys and recovery phrases are never required by the portfolio dashboard.

Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers about NEXUS VAULT, wallet connections, privacy and your portfolio.

What is NEXUS VAULT?

NEXUS VAULT is designed to bring information from your different crypto wallets into one unified portfolio view.

Do I need to share my recovery phrase?

For browser extension connections, no. The import feature is provided for local testing only — the phrase is used in your browser to derive an address and then discarded. Only the address is saved.

Why is Trust Wallet connectable?

Trust Wallet injects its own provider into the browser (either via EIP-6963 or window.trustwallet). NEXUS VAULT detects it directly — no WalletConnect needed on desktop.

How do I connect using QR code?

Click any EVM wallet in Connectable Now, choose "Scan QR Code", and get a free WalletConnect Project ID from cloud.walletconnect.com. Enter it once and the QR code will connect to any WalletConnect-compatible mobile wallet.

Can I connect multiple wallets?

Yes. You can connect MetaMask, Trust Wallet, Phantom, Unisat and other supported wallets at the same time.

Does NEXUS VAULT hold my cryptocurrency?

No. NEXUS VAULT is non-custodial. It only reads public addresses and balances. Your wallet remains in your full control at all times.

Why did transactions fail to load?

Transactions use Blockscout (free, no key required). If Blockscout is rate-limited, add an optional Etherscan API key in Settings for a fallback.

Can I swap tokens here?

Yes. For EVM wallets, NEXUS VAULT uses the 1inch aggregator API (requires a free API key from portal.1inch.dev).

How do I disconnect a wallet?

Open "My Wallets", find the wallet you want to remove, and click Disconnect. This removes the wallet from NEXUS VAULT only — your funds are unaffected.
